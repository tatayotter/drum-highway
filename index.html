Your course

Pass each lesson at full tempo to unlock the next. Each lesson breaks into steps (hands, feet, then slower to full tempo). Each grade ends with an exam (three set pieces, a sight-reading piece and an improvised fill) marked Pass, Merit or Distinction, and passing it opens the next grade. Progress is saved in this browser.

Speed trainer

Each round (or each 4-bar pass in Loop) that scores 90% or better raises the tempo, until you reach the target.

Song

Plays the bars over and over with no break. Start slow (set Tempo to 60–70%): each pass you play at 90% or better speeds it up, until 100%.

Practice tools

Wait mode is for learning a new part: there's no backing or click, and the highway holds at each note until you hit the right drum. On the keyboard, hold Shift to play an accent, and press Q for an open hi-hat.

Drum sounds

Pick your sample folder once. The best one-shot for each drum is chosen by file name; change any of them below. Your picks are remembered in this browser.

Using built-in synth drums.

Latency

Calibrate plays 16 clicks. Hit any pad (or key) exactly with each click you hear, and the timing offset is set for you.

Companion app

Optional Windows app that plays your drum samples with very low latency (ASIO / WASAPI) and sends your hits here. Only needed if you want the computer to make the drum sounds.

Pad mapping

MIDI note numbers your kit sends for each lane, comma separated. Click Learn then hit the pad to add its note. Saved in this browser.
